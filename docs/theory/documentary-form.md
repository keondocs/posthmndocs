### On Form as Argument

The way a film is made is itself a claim. Form is not the container for content — it *is* the content.

Duration as respect: to hold a shot longer than is comfortable is to refuse the audience's desire for summary. Non-linearity as epistemological honesty: the world does not unfold in three acts. The essay film as thinking aloud — meaning constructed in the act of looking, not before it.

### Installation as Mode

The gallery or installation context changes the temporality of documentary. The viewer is no longer captured — they move, return, leave. The work must be structured for partial encounters.

Multi-channel installation introduces *simultaneity*: multiple images at once, no single correct viewing order. This is not chaos. It is a different epistemology.

### On the Cut

Every cut is a decision about what can follow what — about causality, proximity, rhythm. The cut is not invisible technique. It is the film's primary argument.
