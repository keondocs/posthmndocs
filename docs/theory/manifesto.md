### Statement

Documentary is not observation. It is intervention. Every lens position is a claim about what matters, who matters, and what understanding means.

There is no innocent camera. There is no neutral cut.

### Mission

To help people better understand themselves, each other, and their place in the world — by reshaping not only the *content* of documentary but its *form and practice* itself.

### The Posthuman Lens

The boundary between subject and filmmaker is permeable, contested, generative. The documentary does not capture a world that pre-exists it. It participates in the making of worlds.

This means:

- The filmmaker is not outside the story
- The archive is a living body, not a static record
- The edit is a form of thought
- Duration is a form of respect
- Silence is a form of speaking

### Against the Dominant Gaze

Who gets to speak? Who gets seen? Whose interiority is granted complexity, and whose is reduced to function?

Posthuman Docs works against the extractive logic of documentary-as-representation. The goal is not to show people. It is to *think with* them.

### Method

Work from the inside-out. Stay with the trouble. Let form follow ethics, not convention.
