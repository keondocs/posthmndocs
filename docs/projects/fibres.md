### Concept

Fibres is a documentary installation exploring the invisible labor of care through the act of eating. Food as connective tissue — between bodies, ecosystems, memory, and exhaustion.

Care is not supplementary to life. It *is* life, maintained. Fibres asks: what does it mean to feed someone? What worlds are sustained — and which ones collapse — in the quiet work of the kitchen?

### Form

Multi-screen installation. Long-duration takes. Non-verbal sequences. The camera lingers where language fails — in the grain of a hand, the steam above a pot, the silence of a shared meal.

Sound design takes precedence over voice. The documentary refuses the talking-head as its organizing structure.
