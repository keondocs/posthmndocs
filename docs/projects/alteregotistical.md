### Concept

A video essay series on the alter ego as a tool of transformation — not performance, but a genuine mechanism for becoming. The alter ego is not a mask worn over the self. It is the self, refusing its own limitations.

Drawing on Nietzsche's self-overcoming, Jung's shadow integration, and the villain archetype as a site of radical self-possession.

### Theoretical Anchors

- **Byung-Chul Han** — the achievement subject; the fatigue of self-optimization
- **Baudrillard** — the simulacrum of the self; identity as image that precedes its referent
- **Nietzsche** — *Übermensch* as becoming beyond the given; will as creative force
- **Jung** — the shadow as the reservoir of what the ego refuses; integration as wholeness

### Archetypal Reference

The Bleach archetype runs through this series: Aizen Sōsuke as total self-possession — no gap between will and act. Ichigo as the figure who must integrate his shadow (Zangetsu) to become whole. The villain not as moral failure but as structural refusal of false limits.

### Form

Hybrid essay film. Archival footage, screen recordings, original footage. Voice as instrument — not narration but thinking-aloud.

### Status

In active development. Phase 1: research and scripting.
